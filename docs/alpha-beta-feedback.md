## UX Feedback Categories

### Navigation
- Was it easy to find what you were looking for?
- Did the Back button work as expected?
- Were the menus clear?

### Registration & Login
- Was creating an account smooth?
- Did the verification email arrive quickly?
- Was the login process easy?

### Booking Flow
- Was it clear how to book a service?
- Did the scheduling picker work well?
- Was the pricing breakdown clear?
- Did the GCash payment work?

### Performance
- Did pages load quickly?
- Did the app feel smooth or laggy?
- Did the app crash at any point?
- How was it on slow internet?

### Design
- Was text easy to read?
- Were buttons easy to tap?
- Did the layout look good on your phone?
- Were there any overlapping elements?

## How to Submit Feedback

1. **During testing**: Take screenshots and note the steps
2. **Submit via**: 
   - Google Forms link (to be shared by the test coordinator)
   - Or email to: support@serbisyogo.ph
   - Or through the in-app Support page
3. **Include**: Your device model, Android version, and screenshots

## Severity Guide

When reporting issues, mark the severity:

| Severity | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Critical** | App crashes or data loss | Cannot register, payment fails silently |
| 🟠 **Major** | Feature doesn't work | Cannot complete booking, messages don't send |
| 🟡 **Minor** | Cosmetic or minor issue | Text overlaps, wrong alignment |
| 🟢 **Suggestion** | Improvement idea | "Would be nice if..." |
